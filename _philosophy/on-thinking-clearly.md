Here begins the **Philosophy & Thinking** section. Where the tech notes are
about *how*, these are about *why*.

> The unexamined life is not worth living.

I find that writing forces a kind of honesty that thinking alone does not. When
an idea has to survive contact with a sentence, the vague ones fall apart and
the real ones get sharper.

So this corner of the site is deliberately unhurried — no deadlines, no
audience to perform for, just an attempt to see a little more clearly.
