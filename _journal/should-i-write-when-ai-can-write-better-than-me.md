---
title: "Should I write when AI can write better than me?"
date: 2026-11-02
---

Recently I have been struggling to convince myself to write. If AI can write better than me, then do I need to write myself? Originally I had planned for this website to be a tech + personal blog. I have come to realise, AI can far better explain tech to who wants to learn. But emotions, that's a different matter altogether. Emotions are personal. AI would never be able to "related". It can also give a fake understanding of "understanding emotions".

And this blog will also help me get my writing better. And get and thoughts in order. I'll keep it simple and to the point here. No judgment. We all are unique and deal with emotions in our own way. 

This is my personal argument to writing in the AI world.
